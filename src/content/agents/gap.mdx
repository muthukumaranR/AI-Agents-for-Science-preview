The Gap Analysis / Gap Search Agent is a Simple Agent designed through the AKD-CARE process. It does not require custom APIs, MCP servers, or external system integrations. Instead, it operates through carefully structured prompts, validated reasoning steps, domain-specific instructions, and HITL oversight.

The Gap Agent systematically analyzes a curated literature corpus to surface defensible research gaps, knowledge contradictions, and candidate research questions. It is designed to support scientists in identifying where knowledge is missing or contested without prescribing conclusions — all interpretive decisions remain with the researcher.
