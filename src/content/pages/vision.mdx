Accelerated Knowledge Discovery (AKD) enhances the role of artificial intelligence (AI) capabilities in scientific workflows from a basic utility to a full cognitive partner. By integrating specialized AI Agents into scientific processes, AKD aims to enable a **Closed-Loop Scientific Workflow (CLSW)** in which AI helps scientists to reason through various stages of a scientific workflow to accelerate the pace of science itself.

The **AI Agents for Science** initiative aspires to design and develop a thriving ecosystem of harmonized agentic AI components to broadly support science communities via open collaborations and contributions.

---

### Strategic Objectives

- **Reclaiming Scientist Time:** Currently, scientists spend 70–80% of their time on data munging and wrangling with data and information. AKD reduces friction in these tasks to focus human intellect on high-level hypothesis, experimentation and analysis.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Orchestration:** Instead of siloed searches for information and knowledge, AKD acts as an intelligent orchestrator, connecting disparate data from various science repositories and publications into a unified knowledge stream.
- **Harmonized AI Integration:** Harmonizes the use of AI Science agents across NASA's existing data repositories with community-accepted design processes as well as the use of a common AI Agent SDK with built-in science guardrails.

---

### Technical & Ethical Guardrails

- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** Design process maintains human oversight at appropriate gates to ensure AI remains a partner, not a replacement.
- **Scientific Integrity:** Built on foundations of Transparency, Reproducibility, and Open Science.
- **Science Driven Design Innovation:** Features unique components like the CARE process for agent design, specialized Science Guardrails, and FactReasoner to mitigate hallucinations and ensure factual accuracy.

---

### Software Deliverables

1. **AI Agent Service Layer to support Science Enterprise:** A centralized, cloud-native service providing a set of "Common Agents" pre-configured for different scientific tasks (data/code search, science guardrails, factreasoner, etc.).
2. **Developer Tooling & Integration — AKD AI Agent SDK:** An open source developer toolkit including libraries, documentation, and authentication protocols, enabling developers to build, test, and deploy custom agents that interface with data systems.
3. **Reference Implementations & Demonstrators — AI-Augmented [X] Research Lab:** A fully integrated, "Art of the Possible" pilot deployment within specific domains. This demonstrators serves as a functional blueprint for the Closed-Loop Scientific Workflow (CLSW) and as a "reference implementation" of a new Cyber Infrastructure (CI) for science.
