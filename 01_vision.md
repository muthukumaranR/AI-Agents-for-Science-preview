## Vision

AKD transitions AI from a basic utility to a full cognitive partner in scientific workflows. By integrating specialized AI Agents into the scientific process, AKD aims to enable **"Closed-Loop Scientific Workflow" (CLSW)** where AI helps reason through various stages of the scientific workflow to accelerate science.

AI Agents for science aims to establish a thriving ecosystem to harmonize the design and development of AI Agents for science to support the science community via open collaborations and contributions.

---

### Strategic Objectives

- **Reclaiming Scientist Time:** Currently, scientists spend 70–80% of their time on manual effort. AKD reduces friction in these tasks to focus human intellect on high-level hypothesis and analysis.
- **Cross-Disciplinary Orchestration:** Instead of siloed searches, AKD acts as an intelligent orchestrator, connecting disparate data from various science repositories and publications into a unified knowledge stream.
- **Uniform Infrastructure Integration:** Harmonizes the use of AI Science agents across NASA's existing data repositories with community-accepted design processes as well as the use of a common AI Agent SDK with built-in science guardrails.

---

### Technical & Ethical Guardrails

- **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL):** Design process maintains rigorous oversight to ensure AI remains a partner, not a replacement.
- **Scientific Integrity:** Built on foundations of Transparency, Reproducibility, and Open Science.
- **Proprietary Innovation:** Features unique components like the CARE process for agent design, specialized Science Guardrails, and FactReasoner to mitigate hallucinations and ensure factual accuracy.

---

### Software Deliverables

1. **Centralized AI Agent Service Layer:** A centralized, cloud-native service providing a fleet of "Common Agents" pre-configured for different scientific tasks (data/code search, science guardrails, factreasoner, etc.).
2. **Developer Tooling & Integration — AKD AI Agent SDK:** A comprehensive developer toolkit including libraries, documentation, and authentication protocols, enabling developers to build, test, and deploy custom agents that interface with data systems.
3. **Reference Implementation & Demonstrators — AI-Augmented [X] Research Lab:** A fully integrated, "Art of the Possible" pilot deployment within a specific domain. This serves as a functional blueprint for the Closed-Loop Scientific Workflow (CLSW) and as a "reference implementation" as a new cyberinfrastructure for science.

---

### Process & Governance Deliverables

1. **Technical Best Practices:** A codified set of design and implementation community-accepted best practices and architectural requirements for agent development. Includes mandatory protocols for Science Guardrails, modularity, and error handling.
2. **Collaborative Infrastructure:** A centralized "Agent Hub" or marketplace platform where researchers can share, peer-review, and fork agent templates. Includes pipelines for community contributions and a standardized documentation portal.
3. **Operational & Governance Blueprint:** A community-developed manual defining the roles, responsibilities, and decision-making workflows for AI agent deployment. Establishes Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) checkpoints and ethical compliance pathways for AI-generated scientific outputs.
